How it's built

The technical side the client never sees, but which affects the result most.

01

Fast gallery

Images load in stages — first a low-quality placeholder, then the full version. The page looks ready immediately, while the photos are still downloading.

02

Preserving quality

For a photographer, compression is a sore subject. I use WebP at a high quality setting and keep the detail of the original — only the file size drops.

03

Lightbox

A full-screen view with arrow keys, Esc to close and swipe on mobile. Without needlessly heavy libraries.

04

ImageObject Schema

Structured data on every piece — it shows up better in Google Images and authorship is attributed correctly.

05

Updating content

Adding a new piece is simple — one photo and two lines of text. We hand over instructions.

06

No template lock-in

On portfolio platforms (Wix, Squarespace) everyone's design looks the same. A hand-built site follows your style, not a template's.

04

Questions about this service

Answers to the rest are on the home page — or just write to us.

Why does it cost less than an agency?

The routine part of the process — assembling components, responsive testing, image optimization, meta tags — is accelerated with artificial intelligence. What used to take a week now takes days.

Design and decisions are made by a human, and the code is checked by hand. On top of that we carry no office or middleman costs — you pay for the work, not for bureaucracy.

What's included in the first-year price?

Domain (.ge or .com), hosting, SSL certificate and technical support — 12 months free, with no extra charge.

Renewal from year 2: 49₾/month for a landing page and 99₾/month for a business site and store. Cancel any time, no penalty.

How many photos will you upload?

Up to 40 images are included in the price — optimized and placed in the gallery. More photos are a separate stage, or we'll show you how to add them yourself.

Can I have video?

Yes. The video is uploaded to YouTube or Vimeo and embedded on the site — that keeps the page light. Uploading video directly to the site raises hosting costs and we don't recommend it.

Will my photos be protected?

I'll add right-click blocking and, if you want, a discreet watermark. Complete protection doesn't exist on the internet — nobody can stop someone taking a screenshot — but casual copying becomes harder.

Is a domain necessary?

Not necessary, but worth it. name.ge or name.com costs 30-60₾ a year and changes how professional you look, dramatically.
