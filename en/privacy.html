Privacy Policy

In short: we collect only what you type into the brief form yourself. We share it with nobody. We don't use cookies.

Last updated: August 2026

01What we collect

Data is collected on the site only if you fill in the brief form yourself:

Name · phone or email · the service and budget range you chose · the text of your message.

We ask for nothing else. An ID number, a bank card or a password never are things we need — if someone asks for them in our name, it isn't us.

02What we use it for

Only to reply to your enquiry and agree the project with you.

We don't send marketing mailshots. We won't put you on a list.

03Who we share it with

Nobody. We don't sell your data, don't rent it out and don't pass it to third parties.

The only exception is a request provided for by law.

04How the data reaches us

After you fill it in, the brief form opens WhatsApp with the text already filled in — you press send yourself. So the message arrives via WhatsApp and WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to it.

If you write to us via Messenger or Instagram, Meta's policy applies.

05Cookies and analytics

Website does not use cookies to track users.

To see the overall number of visits we may use Cloudflare Web Analytics — it leaves no cookies and does not identify an individual person. Only aggregate statistics are visible: how many visits there were, which page was opened, where it came from.

If we run an advertising campaign, we may add Meta Pixel or Google Ads Tag. In that case this page will be updated and a consent notice will appear on the site.

06How long we keep it

We keep the correspondence for as long as the project requires, plus a reasonable period for subsequent support.

If we don't end up working together, the correspondence stays in the messenger, where you can delete it at any time.

07Your rights

You can ask us at any time for: what data we hold about you, its correction or complete deletion.

Get in touch and we'll reply within 7 working days.

08Security

The site runs on HTTPS with an SSL certificate. The form has spam protection.

The site has no user database or password store — there is nothing held that could be breached.

09Contact

Any question about privacy: 555 53 31 08 or WhatsApp.

When this policy is updated, the date at the top of the page changes.

Still have a question?

Anything not written here, just ask us directly — we go through everything in detail at the brief.
