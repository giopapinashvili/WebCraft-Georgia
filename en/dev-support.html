04

Questions about this service

Answers to the rest are on the home page — or just write to us.

Why does it cost less than an agency?

The routine part of the process — assembling components, responsive testing, image optimization, meta tags — is accelerated with artificial intelligence. What used to take a week now takes days.

Design and decisions are made by a human, and the code is checked by hand. On top of that we carry no office or middleman costs — you pay for the work, not for bureaucracy.

What's included in the first-year price?

Domain (.ge or .com), hosting, SSL certificate and technical support — 12 months free, with no extra charge.

Renewal from year 2: 49₾/month for a landing page and 99₾/month for a business site and store. Cancel any time, no penalty.

What happens if I go over the limit?

I warn you and tell you what the extra volume costs — usually 25-40₾ per hour. You will never receive a bill after the fact.

Is adding a new page included?

Adding a small section — yes. A completely new page is separate work (80-120₾), because it requires design and structure.

Are hosting and the domain included?

First year: domain, hosting and SSL are fully included — you pay nothing extra.

From year 2: hosting and SSL are part of the monthly plan. The domain's annual fee (30-60₾) is separate and you pay it directly to the registrar, so the domain stays under your control.

What if I break the site or mess something up?

That's what backups are for. You can roll back to any point in the 30-day history — free and within the plan.

Does the site stay if I cancel?

Of course. The site is yours, the domain is in your name and all the access is with you. Ending support ends our service, not your site.
