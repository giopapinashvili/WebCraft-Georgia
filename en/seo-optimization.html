02

How it's built

The technical side the client never sees, but which affects the result most.

01

Real data

We write the audit from PageSpeed Insights, Search Console and manual checking — not from an automated scanner's PDF that everyone sends everyone.

02

Images — the biggest win

In 9 cases out of 10 it's images that slow a site down. A 2MB PNG becomes an 80KB WebP with no visible difference. That single change often makes the page twice as fast.

03

Structured data

JSON-LD markup that tells Google what your business is, what services you offer and what they cost. It raises the chance of an expanded result appearing in search.

04

Indexing check

Often a site isn't on Google because robots.txt or a noindex tag is blocking it. That's the very first thing we check.

05

Mobile usability

Google builds its index from the mobile version. If the buttons or the text are too small on a phone, it affects ranking.

06

A report in plain language

Every item is explained in human terms: what the problem was, why it was a problem, what was done and what result should follow.

04

Questions about this service

Answers to the rest are on the home page — or just write to us.

Why does it cost less than an agency?

The routine part of the process — assembling components, responsive testing, image optimization, meta tags — is accelerated with artificial intelligence. What used to take a week now takes days.

Design and decisions are made by a human, and the code is checked by hand. On top of that we carry no office or middleman costs — you pay for the work, not for bureaucracy.

What's included in the first-year price?

Domain (.ge or .com), hosting, SSL certificate and technical support — 12 months free, with no extra charge.

Renewal from year 2: 49₾/month for a landing page and 99₾/month for a business site and store. Cancel any time, no penalty.

How soon will I see results?

The technical improvement (speed, PageSpeed score) is visible immediately. Indexing refreshes within 3-14 days.

Movement on competitive keywords takes 1-3 months and depends on content too, not only on the technical side.

Will you promise me first place on Google?

No — and anyone who promises you that is lying. Nobody controls Google's algorithm.

What I do promise: the site will be technically correct, fast and indexable. That is the foundation, and without it talking about rankings is meaningless.

What's the difference between 299₾ SEO and more expensive SEO?

299₾ is a one-off technical optimization — the part that gets fixed once.

Agencies' monthly SEO covers writing content, building links and monitoring competitors. That's a different service and a different budget.

Can it be a site I didn't build with you?

Yes, any site — WordPress, Wix, Shopify or hand-written. Some platforms have more limitations, and I'll say so plainly in the audit.
