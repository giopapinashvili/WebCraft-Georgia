02

How it's built

The technical side the client never sees, but which affects the result most.

01

Conversion structure

The order of the sections is not random: first the problem, then the solution, then the proof, and finally the action. It's a classic model and it works.

02

One action

On a landing page even a menu is surplus — it leads the visitor away. Navigation is minimal and every link goes to the same button.

03

Fast loading

One page, minimal resources, images in WebP and lazy loading. A visitor arriving from an ad will not wait 3 seconds.

04

Pixel and tracking

Meta Pixel, Google Ads Tag or Analytics — installed, with events (form submissions, button clicks) recorded so you can measure the effect of your ads.

05

Ready for A/B testing

Changing the headline or the button text takes 5 minutes — you can run two versions and see which one works.

06

Spam protection

A honeypot and validation on the form — bot submissions don't come through, so you don't lose real leads in the junk.

03

When a business needs a landing page

If any one of these is about you — this service is for you.

  • You're running Facebook or Google ads and the traffic needs a page to land on
  • You have one specific product or servicethat you're actively selling
  • a seasonal promotion or an event and a full website would be overkill
  • You're testing a new idea and want to see whether there's demand at all
  • You want one link for your Instagram bio that has everything on it

04

Questions about this service

Answers to the rest are on the home page — or just write to us.

Why does it cost less than an agency?

The routine part of the process — assembling components, responsive testing, image optimization, meta tags — is accelerated with artificial intelligence. What used to take a week now takes days.

Design and decisions are made by a human, and the code is checked by hand. On top of that we carry no office or middleman costs — you pay for the work, not for bureaucracy.

What's included in the first-year price?

Domain (.ge or .com), hosting, SSL certificate and technical support — 12 months free, with no extra charge.

Renewal from year 2: 49₾/month for a landing page and 99₾/month for a business site and store. Cancel any time, no penalty.

Aren't a landing page and a website the same thing?

No. A website delivers information — a landing page asks for action. A landing page has fewer choices, fewer links and one clear button. That's exactly why it converts better.

Can I add pages to a landing page later?

Yes — a landing page can grow into a full business website. You pay the difference (999₾ − 249₾ = 750₾) and I add the remaining pages within the existing design.

Will you also help me set up ads?

We prepare the landing page technically for advertising — Pixel, events, speed. Running the campaign itself is a separate service and we discuss it at the brief.

How many leads will I get?

Nobody can promise you a specific number — the result depends on the offer, the price, the ad budget and the market. What I answer for is that the page is built correctly, technically and structurally.
