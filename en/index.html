    06

    Frequently asked questions

    If the answer isn't here — write to us, we'll reply within 24 hours.

    Why is your price half the market rate?

    Two reasons. First — the routine part of the process (assembling components, responsive testing, image optimization, meta tags) is accelerated with AI. What used to take a week now takes days.

    Second — we have no overheads: no office, no project manager, no sales department. You pay for the work, not for bureaucracy.

    If AI writes the code, doesn't quality suffer?

    AI is a tool, not the author. Structure, design and decisions are made by a human, and the code is checked by hand at every stage — on mobile, tablet and desktop.

    You can check the result yourself: our work includes booking systems, admin panels and multilingual sites. The links are open — go and look.

    What's included in the first-year price?

    Domain (.ge or .com), hosting, SSL certificate and technical support — 12 months free, with no extra charge.

    Renewal from year 2: 49₾/mo (or 499₾/year) for a landing page and 99₾/mo (or 999₾/year) for a website and store. Cancel any time, no penalty.

    Will the domain and site be mine?

    Yes. The domain is registered in your name and every access stays with you. The site is your property — if you hand it to another developer tomorrow, everything is available. Nothing is locked.

    How long does a site take?

    Landing page — 2-3 days. Business website — 7 days. Online store — 14 days. The clock starts once I receive the texts and photos; that is most often what stretches the timeline.

    Will I be able to manage the content myself?

    The Business Website and the store include a CMS — you change text, photos and products yourself. We hand over a short set of instructions.

    If you'd rather not deal with it — on a support plan we make the changes, you just tell us what to change.

    How does payment work?

    50% at the start, 50% before launch. Bank transfer. The price is fixed after the brief and does not grow during the project — if the scope grows, we ask you first and only then do it.

    What if I don't like the design?

    We agree the design before any code is written — precisely for this reason. At that stage changes are free and fast. After the code, 2 rounds of revisions are still included in the price.
